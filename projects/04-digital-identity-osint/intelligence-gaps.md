# Intelligence Gaps — OSINT-004

**Scenario:** Fictional training investigation  
**Purpose:** Document missing information that limits analytical confidence.

## 1. Identified Intelligence Gaps

### IG-01: Independent proof of account control
No verified account-owner records are available.

**Impact:** We cannot confirm that the same person controls all three accounts.

### IG-02: Origin of the shared phrase
There is no independent evidence showing whether the phrase was original, copied, or taken from a common source.

**Impact:** The shared phrase may be less significant than it initially appears.

### IG-03: Authenticity of the profile link
The scenario provides no historical snapshot or independent capture of the link between Accounts A and C.

**Impact:** The link suggests a relationship but does not establish who created it or controls either account.

### IG-04: Timestamp verification
The timestamps are provided by the fictional scenario and have not been independently verified.

**Impact:** The sequence of events cannot be independently confirmed.

### IG-05: Alternative explanations
Possible explanations include impersonation, shared community membership, copied text, or participation in a common discussion.

**Impact:** The available evidence cannot exclude these alternatives.

### IG-06: Independent corroboration
Several indicators may be related rather than independent.

**Impact:** Counting every similarity as separate supporting evidence could exaggerate the strength of the assessment.

## 2. Priority Collection Requirements

For an authorized real-world investigation, appropriate next steps could include:

- Independently capturing relevant public records.
- Verifying publication dates and timestamps.
- Checking whether the phrase or technical topic originated from a shared public source.
- Seeking independent corroboration before making account-ownership claims.

These are proposed requirements only; no real-world collection was performed.

## 3. What Cannot Be Concluded

- Common control of all three accounts has not been confirmed.
- No real-world identity has been verified.
- No real person or group has been attributed to the accounts.
- The lack of confirming evidence does not prove that the accounts are unrelated.

## 4. Assessment

The identified gaps prevent a high-confidence conclusion about common ownership. The accounts may be related, but further independent evidence would be required to support a stronger assessment.
