## Evidence Handling Rules

1. Record observations separately from interpretations.
2. Do not treat a matching username as proof of common ownership.
3. Consider whether evidence items may be dependent on one another.
4. Document alternative explanations.
5. Do not claim that private information was obtained.
6. Use only the confidence level justified by the total evidence.
