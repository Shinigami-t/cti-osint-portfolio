# Project 04: Digital Identity / Persona OSINT

## Case Overview

**Case ID:** OSINT-004  
**Analyst:** Shinigami-t  
**Status:** Simulated investigation  
**Scenario:** Fictional  
**Assessment date:** 28 October 2026

## Objective

Assess whether three fictional online accounts can reasonably be linked to the same online persona using simulated, publicly observable evidence.

## Investigation Questions

1. Do the accounts share distinctive identifiers?
2. Are the observed similarities independent and reliable?
3. Could the similarities have reasonable alternative explanations?
4. What confidence level is justified by the available evidence?

## Scope

This exercise uses fictional accounts and simulated observations. It does not investigate real people, access private accounts, bypass access controls, or attempt to identify an actual person's offline identity.

## Methodology

The investigation will use an evidence register, source reliability assessment, timeline, correlation analysis, and explicit documentation of intelligence gaps.

## Important Limitation

This is a training scenario, not a real-world investigation. All account details and observations are fictional and must not be presented as genuine intelligence.
