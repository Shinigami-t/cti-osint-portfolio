# Initial Assessment — Operation Glass Finch

## Preliminary judgment

The reported email is likely a phishing attempt. It uses a threat of mailbox suspension to pressure the recipient into verifying their account through a supplied link.

## Supporting observations

- The email uses urgent language and threatens account suspension.
- The sender claims to represent corporate IT support.
- The employee reports being directed to a sign-in page after clicking the link.

## Potential impact

The employee reports closing the page without entering credentials. Therefore, credential disclosure has not been established. Further investigation is required before concluding that the account and device are unaffected.

## Intelligence gaps

1. Original email headers, including sender-routing information and authentication results.
2. Relevant mail gateway, web-proxy and authentication logs.
3. Available information about the URL and its associated infrastructure.

## Confidence and limitations

This is a preliminary assessment based on a fictional incident brief and employee reporting. The original email headers, infrastructure records and server logs have not been provided. No threat actor attribution has been established.
