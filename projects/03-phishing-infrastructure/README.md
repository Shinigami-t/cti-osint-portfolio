# Project 03 — Phishing Infrastructure Investigation

## Overview

This simulated CTI investigation examines a reported phishing email, its associated URL, potential exposure risks, and the evidence needed to assess the incident responsibly.

All entities, indicators, and events in this case are fictional. The `.example` domain is reserved for documentation.

## Objectives

- Assess suspicious email characteristics.
- Separate reported observations from verified technical evidence.
- Identify potential phishing and account-exposure risks.
- Define evidence requests for email, DNS, and authentication investigations.
- Document uncertainty and avoid unsupported attribution.

## Project Contents

### Evidence
- `evidence/incident-brief.txt` — Fictional incident report and preserved indicators.

### Analysis
- `analysis/initial-assessment.md` — Preliminary incident assessment.
- `analysis/indicator-analysis.md` — Assessment of the supplied indicators.
- `analysis/evidence-requests.md` — Proposed additional evidence collection.
- `analysis/timeline.md` — Reported event sequence and timeline limitations.
- `analysis/exposure-assessment.md` — Potential exposure risks and recommended evidence.

## Key Analytical Principle

A suspicious email and a reported link visit do not, by themselves, prove credential theft, successful compromise, or session theft. Conclusions must reflect the available evidence.

## Limitations

This is a fictional training exercise. No live phishing infrastructure was contacted, and no real email headers, DNS records, endpoint telemetry, or authentication logs were collected.
