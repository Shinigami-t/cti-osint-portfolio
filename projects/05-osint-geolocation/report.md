# Image Geolocation Investigation Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This investigation examines a photograph for clues that may establish its geographic location. The case remains in progress. Kentucky is a working hypothesis, but the exact location has not been conclusively verified.

## 2. Scope
The investigation includes image metadata inspection, visual clue collection, candidate identification, and planned independent corroboration.

## 3. Metadata Findings
ExifTool 13.50 identified the file as a JPEG image measuring 2763 x 1842 pixels. Available metadata identifies a Sony SLT-A57 camera and records an original timestamp of 21 October 2012 at 12:38:01. No GPS latitude or longitude fields appeared in the inspected output.

These metadata fields do not independently identify the location.

## 4. Visual Findings
The analyst reported observing:
- A sign reading "FAILTE".
- A horse graphic and wording mentioning Kentucky.
- Power lines near the store.
- A nearby car park.
- A small grassy planted area with pots.

These observations have not all been independently verified against the original image at sufficient detail.

## 5. Hypotheses
Kentucky is a candidate region based on the reported wording and horse graphic. Lexington and a possible Irish-themed shop were suggested during preliminary research.

Neither the exact business nor the exact photographic location is confirmed.

## 6. Source and Method Limitations
The photograph came from a publicly accessible source whose page reportedly includes location information. Consequently, this case cannot be presented as a fully blind discovery.

The original source URL, licence, publication context, and any externally published coordinates must be documented before the case is finalized.

## 7. Outstanding Work
1. Verify the original image source and licence.
2. Record exact, readable sign wording and other distinctive features.
3. Identify candidate businesses using reliable sources.
4. Compare candidate storefronts and surroundings with the target photograph.
5. Record citations and explain how each source supports or contradicts the hypothesis.
6. Assign a confidence level only after corroboration.

## 8. Preliminary Conclusion
The available observations provide leads for further research but do not yet establish an exact location. The case remains open.
